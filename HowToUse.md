# How to use
Farming++ has a total of 6 modes in it's OLED that lets you control the system:
1. Welcome screen - greets you when you open the system
2. Temperature screen - shows the temperature
3. Humidity screen - shows the Humidity
4. Soil Moisture screen - shows the Moisture present in soil
5. AI screen - shows the AI part of the app. Once opened, you can question the AI. The AI can control the motors and has knowledge of the climate near you. You can make him do so by telling "Turn Motor 1/2 on/off".
